New here? A 20-second guide
  • HB / SB are House and Senate bills. HJR / SJR are proposed constitutional amendments, which voters must approve.
  • The caption is the bill's official one-sentence summary ("Relating to…"). The full text is usually posted within hours of filing.
  • The author is the member who filed the bill. Coauthors are members of the same chamber who sign on later.
  • Pick a topic on the left to filter everything. Use Bills to read them, Map to see where they come from, and Network to see who files together.

    How the map is built

    A district's shade is the number of bills its member has filed as an author. A bill with several authors counts once for each of them. Authors are matched to districts by name using a member roster that includes those elected in November 2026. District lines come from the Census Bureau.

    How the network is built

    A line joins two members who put their names on the same bill: the author with each joint author and coauthor. Thicker lines mean more shared bills, and bigger dots mean more bills authored. Few bills have coauthors this early, so expect it to fill in as session nears.

    How topics are assigned

    Each bill gets one main topic from a keyword scorer; no AI model is involved. It weighs the caption most, then the words in the full text and which Texas law codes the bill amends. Until the text is posted, only the caption is used, and the bill is re-scored once it arrives. Against the Legislature's own subject labels the main topic agrees nearly 9 times in 10, so treat it as a guide and check the bill.
