TEXAS FILING MONITOR

relating to how this site works.

Section 1. Source

The site reads the public bill files that the Texas Legislature publishes for the 90th Regular Session. It checks them about every 15 minutes and records each bill's number, caption, authors, filing date and introduced text. A new bill's caption can trail its filing by up to an hour, because the Legislature publishes that record on its own schedule.

Section 2. Topics

A keyword scorer assigns each bill one main topic and up to two secondary topics. It weighs the caption most, then the words in the bill's text and the Texas codes the bill amends. Until the Legislature posts a bill's text, the caption alone sets the topic, and the site scores the bill again when the text arrives.

The topics are unofficial. Measured against the Legislature's own subject labels, the main topic agrees for nearly nine bills in ten, so treat a topic as a guide and read the bill.

Section 3. Map

The map shades each district by the number of bills its member has filed as an author. A bill with several authors counts once in each author's district. The site matches authors to districts by name, using a roster of sitting members and the members elected in November 2026. The Census Bureau supplies the district lines, and OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap contributors supply the base map.

Section 4. Network

A line joins two members who signed the same bill, linking each author to that bill's joint authors and coauthors. Larger marks show members who have authored more bills, and thicker lines show pairs who share more of them.
